# Product pitch

Pulse is a watchlist built as an inbox. A price grid shows the same thing
forever, so people stop reading it. Pulse shows only what changed since you last
looked, ranked by whether it earned your attention.

Thresholds are measured in each symbol's own dispersion using robust statistics,
so a 3% day is remarkable for Nestlé and routine for Vodafone Idea. Signals
combine by noisy-OR and decay, so an untouched inbox goes quiet rather than
accumulating red.

Two convictions shaped the rest: degraded feeds are normal, so staleness and
source conflict are shown, not smoothed; and ten correlated names are not ten
bets.
